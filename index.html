The Subah collection

Six pieces in lawn, linen and washed cotton. Pick a colour, then add it to your bag.

Noor means light. We cut clothes to meet it.

Aly Noor started with a simple idea: clothes should look their best at nine in the morning, not just under shop lights. We choose fabrics that fall softly in daylight and keep every shape easy to wear and easy to wash.

Each piece in the Subah collection is named for a part of the day or a kind of light: Fajr for first light, Dhoop for sunshine, Saaya for shade, Shaam for evening, Kiran for a single ray.
